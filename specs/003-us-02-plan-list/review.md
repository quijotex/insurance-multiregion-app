### Specific checks requested

The doc comment links ADR 0005. `agesFromStep1` reuses `ageOn` (the same logic as `legalAge`), the vehicle age is `max(0, year − vehicleYear)`, and invalid values give null. The seed bands match data-model.md. Test gaps: R-11.
- **Currency formatting: conforms**, except R-05 and R-12. `Intl.NumberFormat(numberLocale, { style: 'currency', currency, min/maxFractionDigits: 0 })` plus " / mes". UT-03 covers COP and CLP.
- **`step1CompleteGuard` (R8): conforms**, except R-02. Every `inject()` runs before the first `await`. It uses `restoredValue` and `buildValidators` on a throw-away group, clears the completion when the values are invalid or the fields fail to load, and redirects to step 1. **`planChosenGuard`: conforms** (sync; the summary route runs both guards).

- **Plan save check: conforms in code** (create, update and publish; `{ connect: [], disconnect: [] }` counts as untouched; `set: []` and `disconnect` clear it; numeric ids are resolved; the stored draft is checked on publish). The test is weak: R-10.

- **Seed and backfill:** `seedPlans` is idempotent (it skips when any plan exists), publishes the plans, and links them by country `documentId`. It has 3 CO + 3 CL plans and 1 inactive one (CO "Clásico"), with one recommended plan per country. The backfill is **not** safe: R-01.
- **Plan card accessibility:** a native radio group in a `<fieldset>`/`<legend>`; the arrow keys move the choice (Swiper's Keyboard module ignores focused inputs, and `choose()` calls `slideTo`). The recommended plan is announced (R-06 spacing). There is a focus ring through `:has(:focus-visible)`. Selection causes no layout shift (the ring is 1px border + 1px inset shadow, and the radio fill is the non-colour cue). The recommended top border is offset by −4px padding. The whole card is the click target, and the nav arrows are 48px. Open issues: R-07 (dots), R-09 (label content).
- **Swiper lifecycle: conforms.** `afterRenderEffect` mounts it only when the slider breakpoint matches and offers exist, and unmounts it otherwise (`destroy(true, true)`) and on destroy. The `matchMedia` listener is removed. `loop: false`, no autoplay, `initialSlide` = the chosen plan, else the recommended one. Arrows and dots render only in slider mode. Reduced motion: R-08.
- **Tokens and Bootstrap: conforms**, except R-13. There is no raw hex or `rgba` in the new SCSS. The px literals (14px radius, 6 × 18 px badge padding) are recipe values. Templates use only layout utilities (`row`, `col-*`, `d-flex`, `flex-*`, `justify-content-*`, `gap-3`, `py-4`) and `visually-hidden`.
- **Tests vs approval:** UT-01…UT-10 all exist, under `US-02 · FR-XXX` names. No unapproved test was added. The other spec changes only update the `Country` fixtures for the new fields. E2E-3 and E2E-4 exist and match the approved flows.
