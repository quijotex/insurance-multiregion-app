## US-00 – Country selection

### Story

As a person interested in insuring my car, I want to choose the country where I want
to buy the insurance, so that the form, plans, prices and terms I see apply to my country.

### Context

The same application sells car insurance in Colombia and Chile. Everything the person
sees in the following steps (form fields, plans, currency, terms and conditions) depends
on the country, so the country must be known before the wizard starts. The business team
wants to manage the available countries from Strapi, including their name and flag or
icon, so that a new country can be offered without changing code.

### Business rules

- The available countries are configured in Strapi with: name, code, icon, order and
  active status.
- Only published and active countries are shown, in the configured order.
- Only one country can be selected.
- The selected country is reflected in the address, so a shared link opens the
  application directly in that country, without going through the selection screen.
- If the address contains a country that does not exist or is not active, the person
  sees the selection screen with a message explaining that the country is not available.
- Changing the country after starting the wizard discards the data entered for the
  previous country, after the person confirms it.

### Acceptance criteria

```gherkin
Scenario: Countries available
  Given Colombia and Chile are active and published in Strapi
  When I open the application without a country
  Then I see the country selection screen with Colombia and Chile, in the configured order

Scenario: Country selection
  Given I am on the country selection screen
  When I select Chile
  Then I move on to the data form
  And the form, plans and terms I see are the ones configured for Chile

Scenario: Direct link to a country
  Given I have a link to the application for Colombia
  When I open it
  Then I see the data form for Colombia without going through the selection screen

Scenario: Country not available
  Given the link points to a country that is not active in Strapi
  When I open it
  Then I see the country selection screen
  And I see a message explaining that the country is not available

Scenario: Change of country
  Given I entered data in the form for Colombia
  When I choose to change the country to Chile
  Then I am asked to confirm that my data will be discarded
  And if I confirm, I see the empty form for Chile

Scenario: Keyboard selection
  Given I am on the country selection screen
  When I use only the keyboard
  Then I can move between the countries, select one and continue
```
